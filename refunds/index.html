Refund Policy

If a product doesn't help you, you can have your money back within 14 days.

Within 14 days, in full

Write to support@monaidea.com within 14 days of your purchase, with your order number (it's on your receipt), and we refund you in full. You don't need to explain why.

Once for each product

A refund is once per buyer for each product: if you buy a product again after we refunded it, that purchase can't be refunded.

How you get it

Creem, our reseller, returns the money to the card or account you paid with. How soon it shows depends on your bank; usually within 10 business days.

If we don't answer

We answer within two business days. If we haven't answered within 7 days, you can also contact Creem at creem.io/contact.

Your rights

This policy doesn't limit any right you have under the law where you live, for example if a product is faulty or not as described.
